More than a chatbot

Four tools that work together, so every conversation builds on what MindEase already knows about you.

Personal conversations

Talk it out with a companion that detects your emotions and suggests coping exercises when they'd actually help.

Mood tracking

Quick daily check-ins for mood, energy, sleep and emotions, charted so you can see your trends.

CBT thought records

Write down a stressful thought and get the thinking traps identified, evidence weighed, and a balanced reframe.

Pattern insights

AI looks across two weeks of your data to surface patterns — like how sleep affects your mood — and next steps.
